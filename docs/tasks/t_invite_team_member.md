# Invite a Team Member {#t_invite_team_member .task}

Send an invitation to a colleague so they can access the Syndication Service under your organization.

You must have the Admin or System Administrator role to invite new members.

1.  In the navigation menu, click **Administration**.

2.  Click **Invite Member**.

    The Invite Member dialog opens.

3.  Enter the invitee's email address in the **Email** field.

4.  Select the role to assign to the invitee.

    |Role|Access level|
    |----|------------|
    |**Member**|Can create, configure, and run syncs. Cannot access Administration or manage other users.|
    |**Admin**|All Member access plus the ability to manage members and invitations in Administration.|

5.  Click **Send Invitation**.

    An invitation is sent to the specified email address and appears in the **Invitations** table on the Administration page. The invitee must click the link in the invitation email to complete registration and gain access.


If the invitee does not receive the invitation email, return to the Administration page and use the copy link button on their invitation row to share the invitation link directly.
