# Dashboard {#c_dashboard .concept}

The Dashboard is the home page of the Syndication Service. It gives you an at-a-glance overview of your syncs and recent activity so you can quickly assess the health of your content publishing pipeline.

When you log in to the Syndication Service, the Dashboard is the first page you see. It is organized into two areas: a row of summary tiles at the top and two detail panels below.

## Summary tiles {#section_kpi_tiles .section}

The four tiles across the top of the Dashboard give you an immediate count of key metrics:

Total Syncs
:   The total number of sync configurations in your organization.

Active
:   The number of syncs that are currently enabled and will run on their configured schedule.

Recent Syncs
:   The number of sync runs surfaced in the Recent Syncs panel.

Errors
:   The number of recent runs that ended in a failed or error status. The tile is highlighted when errors are present.

## Syncs panel {#section_syncs_panel .section}

The **Syncs** panel lists your sync configurations. Each row shows a colored status dot,

-   the sync name

-   the source and target systems

-   and the configured schedule.


A green dot indicates the sync is active. A gray dot indicates it is inactive.

Click any sync row to open its detail page. Click **View all** to open the full Syncs list.

**Tip:** If no syncs have been created yet, the panel shows a prompt with a link to create your first sync.

## Recent Syncs panel {#section_recent_runs_panel .section}

The **Recent Syncs** panel shows the most recent sync results across your syncs. Each row shows:

-   the run status

-   the name of the sync that produced it

-   and the time the run started.


When available, it also shows the number of articles changed during that run.

Click **View all** to open the full Sync History page.

## Creating a sync from the Dashboard {#section_new_sync .section}

Click **New Sync** in the upper-right corner of the Dashboard to open the sync creation form.
